## Question

Which closes the cracks between terrain tiles: gap-sized skirts or zipper strips? CS-166's fix for the ×10 cliff bands builds on the answer.

## Options and recommendation

- A. Gap-sized skirts: each edge's skirt is as deep as the drop to the drawn neighbor's edge; matching edges get none.
- B. Zipper strips: no skirts; a strip joins the two edge polylines where neighbors differ.

The spike this item needs measures both and fills in the costs and the recommendation.
