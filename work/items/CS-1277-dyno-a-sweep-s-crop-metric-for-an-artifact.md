> **Proposal.** Source: CS-166 (the ×10 cliff bands), relayed by orch-11 (2026-11-24). Why: the cliff bands turned out to be shadows cast by terrain skirts. A crop metric that compares shadows on with shadows off mostly measures the exposure difference between the two, which swamps the crop's own difference. Size: small. Decider: owner. Filed 2026-11-24.

## Done when

- A sweep's crop metric for an artifact compares against a shadowed setting, such as the same shadows without the artifact's cause, not against shadows off.
- The sweep's record names the reference setting, and a test shows an exposure change alone doesn't move the metric.
