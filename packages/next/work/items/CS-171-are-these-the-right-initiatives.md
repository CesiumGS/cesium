## Question

Seven initiatives are proposed from the overall plan's milestone groupings: renderers and engine profiles; Cesium IR and materials; threading; loading and level of detail; warps and the transform graph; the development harness; upstream follow-ups. Keep, merge or rename?

## Options and recommendation

Recommend keeping them until a second engineer's work shows a better cut. Two are judgement calls: the transform graph could join Cesium IR and materials (its open work is mostly streaming and level of detail under warps), and the upstream follow-ups could be a project under the development harness instead of an initiative of their own.

## Answer

Keep the seven initiatives (owner, 2026-11-13). Revisit when a second engineer's work shows a better cut.
