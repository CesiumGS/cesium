## Question

The planning audit of 2026-11-13 added projects that none of the seven initiatives fits well, and placed each under the closest one: Earth to Moon and the frames follow-ups, and B-reps, under warps and the transform graph (CS-5); the sky, ray queries and the infrastructure capabilities under renderers and engine profiles (CS-1). CS-171 kept the seven; this asks only about the newcomers.

## Options and recommendation

- **One new initiative, "Bodies, frames and engineering coordinates",** for Earth to Moon and the frames follow-ups (recommended: they are a large workstream that isn't about warps).
- **Also a "Rendering features" initiative** for the sky, ray queries and the capabilities.
- **Keep the placements as they are.**

## Answer

Open.
