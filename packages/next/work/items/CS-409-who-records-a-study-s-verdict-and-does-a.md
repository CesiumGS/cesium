## Question

Who records a study's verdict, and does a verdict close a decision item?

## Options and recommendation

- **Recommended:** agents may record verdicts on studies. A verdict that informs a decision item is shown on that item, and closing the decision stays the owner's step in the tracker.
- **Alternative:** only the owner records verdicts. Slower, and agents' conclusions sit in notes until reviewed.

CS-404 and CS-405 build the recommendation unless the owner answers otherwise.
