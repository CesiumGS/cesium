Once the agent system has pages of its own beside the Work page, Dyno is two apps in one: the engine (2.0 against 1.x) and the work (the agent system building it). A switch at the top of the rail swaps the rail between them, each space with its own landing page, groups and counts, as the design note's information architecture plans (option 2, the owner's answer of 2026-11-15; option 1, the work in a group of its own, is in place).

## Done when

- A switch at the top of the rail chooses the engine or the work, and the rail shows that space's pages, groups and counts.
- Each space has its own landing page (the engine's Overview, the work's Work page), and `/` opens the engine's.
- Every route belongs to one space and the rail highlights it; the shortcut letters, the Go to palette and the counts reach both spaces from either.
- The rail test checks each space's groups against the design note's table.
