## Summary

Evidence the 1.x team can check without trusting the agents that wrote the code: independent oracles, measured test strength, adversarial checks, honest accuracy, provenance and human audit, rolled into a trust report per module and per release. It starts at alpha.1 and gates rc.1. As human-in-the-lead matures, its own evals help along the way. The owner, 2026-11-17: "we'll need to convince the existing cesium team that this is production worthy."
