## Summary

One time model for every content source, and one motion model for the engine. 3D Tiles 2.0's time-dynamic tiles are really conditional content, which may be multidimensional or rule-based. The design reads the 3D Tiles 2.0 spec when it starts and fits it with the animation core (CS-211), glTF animation (CS-278), the transform graph, time-bound values, trajectories and articulations as one consistent whole (owner, 2026-11-17).
