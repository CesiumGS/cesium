Tileset parity: CesiumJS 1.x Cesium3DTileset vs 2.0 tile content

Full-detail content of a 3D Tiles tileset (b3dm or glb, with feature IDs and batch/property tables) in both engines from the same camera. 1.x streams it with maximumScreenSpaceError 0 until every tile is loaded; 2.0 walks the tileset (transforms, REPLACE/ADD refinement, external tilesets) and places each content directly. Streaming and tile selection are M2. Also compares picks at a grid of pixels on a feature property. Private ion assets: set VITE_ION_TOKEN in .env.local and open with ?asset=<id>.
